We have released L4 Language extensions for VS Code — the most significant step yet in making L4 accessible to legal engineers who are not computer scientists. The extension provides syntax highlighting, live validation, inline natural language preview, and navigation tools for tracing dependencies between rules. It also integrates with AI assistants to generate draft L4 encodings from plain-language rule descriptions, and to generate plain-language explanations from L4 specifications.

The toolchain has always been the bottleneck. L4's logical foundations are sound — the SMU CCLAW research programme has ensured that. The IDE is the bridge between theoretical soundness and practical use. Legal engineers can now work in L4 with the same tooling support that software engineers expect.

The extension is open source and available on the VS Code marketplace. We expect 2025 to be the year of the first serious production deployments of L4-encoded regulatory frameworks.
