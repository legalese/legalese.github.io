Legalese Cloud runs anything written in L4: your own rules, contracts and policies, or encodings from the [Commons](/commons) library.

- **Deploy a rule as an API.** Export functions from an L4 file and call them over REST, or as tools for AI agents over MCP.
- **Explore in plain language.** An AI chat interface explains what an encoding decides and why, citing the provision it relies on.
- **Free to start.** Sign in to the [Cloud Console](/console) to create your first deployment.

The tutorials are in the L4 documentation: [Legalese Cloud](/l4/tutorials/legalese-cloud).
