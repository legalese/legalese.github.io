Legalese Studio takes a law from its text to a tested, deployable model, without writing code. It works in stages:

- **Design**: scope the law, choose whose questions it answers, and get a quote for the rest.
- **Resolve**: settle the ambiguities in the text, one multiple-choice question at a time, with the reading taken recorded.
- **Refine**: shape the scenarios the model must handle, again by question and answer.
- **Test**: run the cases the text implies, and see which pass.
- **Deploy and Interact**: publish the model to Legalese Cloud and ask it questions in a dynamic form.

Every decision is traced back to the provision it comes from. The model it produces is L4, so it can also be opened and edited in VS Code.
