Legislate.4 is for parliamentary counsel and law revision offices.

- **Logical defect detection**: unreachable provisions, contradictory duties, gaps no branch catches.
- **Drafting manual compliance**: the office's own rules, applied at scale.
- **Consequential amendments** found across the whole statute book.
- **Corpus conversion** to structured, standards-based XML with stable identifiers.

See what an encoded statute book looks like in the [Commons](/commons).
