Prosecute.4 is for prosecuting authorities.

- **Charge selection**: which offences the facts disclose, and which they do not.
- **Elements analysis**: what must be proved, and by whom.
- **Defences** surfaced as defeaters, with the onus correctly placed.
- **Penalty ranges** as bounded consequences rather than free text.
