Comply.4 is for regulated businesses.

- **Obligation mapping**: entity and activity to the duties that attach.
- **Live deadlines**: pending duties tracked as clocks, not checklists.
- **Auditable determinations** citing the subsection relied on.
- **Amendment monitoring**, re-evaluating the positions an amendment affects.
