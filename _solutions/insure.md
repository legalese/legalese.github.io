Insure.4 is for insurers, brokers and regulators.

- **Policy wordings as rules**: what is covered, what is excluded, and what the policyholder must do.
- **The law around the policy**: insurance legislation and consumer protection, encoded alongside the wording.
- **Claims decisions** traced to the clause and the provision relied on.
