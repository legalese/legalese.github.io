Commons is open source at [github.com/legalese/commons](https://github.com/legalese/commons), and encodings are
reviewed like code.

- **Encode.** Take a requirement from a jurisdiction's ledger, or bring a statute you know well. Encodings come with
  the worked cases that test them. Fork the repository, commit to a branch, and open a pull request against `main`.
- **Verify.** Check that an encoding faithfully represents its provision, and say what you checked it against.
- **Challenge.** Disagree with a reading? Open an issue with your authority.
- **Nominate.** Something missing that matters? Open an issue naming the instrument and the question it should answer.

How encodings are written and filed is set out in the repository's `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/directory-conventions.md`.
