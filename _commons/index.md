## How to read Commons

Every instrument in Commons moves through three stages:

- **Indexed**: Commons knows the instrument exists and where its official text is published.
- **Sourced**: the official text is deposited in Commons, with its licence and provenance.
- **Encoded**: some or all of its rules are written in [L4](/l4), with worked cases that test them.

Each jurisdiction has its own page and statute map. The addresses follow one pattern:
`/commons/<jurisdiction>/<type>/<name>`, for example `/commons/au-wa/act/wills-act-1970`. A jurisdiction is a
country code (`au`, `sg`, `nz`) or a country and state code (`au-wa`), the form the Akoma Ntoso legislative
standard uses; Commonwealth law is at `/commons/au`, Western Australian law at `/commons/au-wa`.
