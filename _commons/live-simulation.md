The live simulation follows people through their lives: birth, school, work, marriage, a home, a business, illness,
death. Every event that the law touches is decided by the encoded statute book, not by a script.

When an event needs a rule that is not yet encoded, the simulation does not guess. It records the question as a
requirement in the jurisdiction's ledger, and the requirement sets what is encoded next. The first lives are
scripted in Singapore and Western Australia. Together they have raised more than four hundred such questions,
and most of Singapore's have since been answered by new encodings.

The simulation is in development. This page will carry it live.
